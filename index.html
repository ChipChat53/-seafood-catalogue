Ordering System

  1. Count freezer stock and flag species below 4–6 weeks' reserve.
  2. Refresh product price, stock, size, origin and freight from direct links.
  3. Reject non-Canadian origin except approved Alaskan crab.
  4. Compare CAD/lb of usable seafood including freight.
  5. Use shipping thresholds only when the added product is genuinely useful.
  6. Buy normal-consumption quantities; avoid unnecessary freezer inventory.
  7. Record purchase below and use it as the next baseline.
