Ordering System

  1. Count freezer stock and flag species below 4–6 weeks' reserve.
  2. Refresh product price, stock, size, origin, freight and promotions from direct links.
  3. Reject non-Canadian origin except approved Alaskan crab.
  4. Compare CAD/lb of usable seafood including freight.
  5. Buy Costco staples locally.
  6. Use Caudle's Senior's Day where the checkout discount changes the merchant winner.
  7. Build Coldwater orders around Canadian Atlantic specialty winners.
  8. Use Larry's when quality/availability/order economics justify it.
  9. Record purchase below and use it as the next baseline.
